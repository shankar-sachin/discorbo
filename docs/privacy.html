Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 15, 2026

🔒 Introduction

At Discorbo, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Discorbo Discord bot ("Bot", "Service", "we", "us", or "our").

By using Discorbo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, do not use the Bot.

✅ In short: We only collect minimal data necessary for the Bot to function. Your data is stored locally, never sold, and you can delete it anytime with /clear-my-data.

📊 Information We Collect

1. Automatically Collected Information

When you interact with Discorbo, we automatically collect:

Data Type What We Collect Purpose
User Identifiers Discord User ID, Username, Avatar URL Identify you in commands and leaderboards
Server Information Server ID, Server Name Provide server-specific features
Command Usage Command name, timestamp, parameters Analytics and debugging
Message Content Content you send in commands Process commands (not stored long-term)

2. User-Provided Information

You voluntarily provide information when you:

  • Use commands that require input (e.g., /remind, /afk, /poll)
  • Participate in games and earn scores/achievements
  • Set preferences or custom settings
  • Interact with buttons, dropdowns, or other UI components

3. Game and Activity Data

For game features, we may store:

  • Trivia Scores: Your answers, score, and leaderboard position
  • RPG Data: Quest progress, battle history, inventory, XP, levels
  • Maze Game: Completion times, coins collected, moves made
  • Daily Rewards: Streak count, last claim timestamp
  • Loot & Items: Items collected, rarity, timestamps

4. Preference Data

  • Reminders: Reminder text, scheduled time, channel ID
  • AFK Status: AFK message, timestamp when set
  • Cooldowns: Last command usage time (temporary, cleared after cooldown expires)

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  1. Provide Core Functionality
    • Execute commands you request
    • Display user and server information
    • Manage game state and progress
  2. Enhance User Experience
    • Maintain leaderboards and rankings
    • Track achievements and progress
    • Deliver scheduled features (reminders, timers)
    • Provide personalized responses (AFK messages)
  3. Improve the Bot
    • Analyze command usage to identify popular features
    • Debug errors and fix bugs
    • Optimize performance and reliability
  4. Ensure Security and Compliance
    • Prevent spam and abuse through cooldowns
    • Detect and block malicious activity
    • Comply with legal obligations

⚠️ We will NEVER: Sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing purposes outside of Bot functionality.

🛡️ Data Storage and Security

Where Your Data is Stored

All data is stored locally on our servers in JSON files. We do not use third-party databases or cloud storage services for user data.

How Long We Retain Data

Data Type Retention Period
Game Scores & Progress Indefinitely (until you delete it)
Reminders Until executed or deleted
AFK Status Until you send a message or clear it
Command Logs 30-90 days (for debugging)
Cooldown Data Temporary (cleared after cooldown expires)

Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

  • 🔐 Data stored in protected file systems with restricted access
  • 🔒 No passwords or sensitive credentials are stored
  • 🛡️ Regular security audits and updates
  • ⚡ Secure communication with Discord via HTTPS/WSS
  • 📝 Regular backups to prevent data loss

Note: While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

✊ Your Rights and Choices

Right to Access

You can request a copy of all data we have stored about you. Contact us with your Discord User ID to request your data.

Right to Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Quick Delete: Use /clear-my-data to instantly remove all your personal data from our systems
  • Manual Request: Contact us to request manual deletion
  • What Gets Deleted: Scores, game progress, reminders, AFK status, preferences, and command history

⚠️ Important: Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your leaderboard positions and achievements will be lost.

Right to Opt-Out

You can stop using the Bot at any time by:

  • Simply not using commands
  • Removing the Bot from your server (if you're an admin)
  • Blocking the Bot (prevents DMs and mentions)

GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you are in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Restriction: Request limitation of processing
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

👶 Children's Privacy

Discorbo does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). Discord's Terms of Service require users to be at least 13 years old.

If you believe we have collected information from a child under the minimum age, please contact us immediately, and we will delete the data.

🍪 Cookies and Tracking

We do NOT use cookies or tracking technologies.

Our website (if applicable) may use basic analytics, but the Bot itself does not use cookies, pixels, or other tracking mechanisms beyond what's necessary for Discord API communication.

🤝 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We May Disclose Your Data Only In These Situations:

  • With Your Consent: If you explicitly agree to share your data
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, court order, or government request
  • Security Threats: To prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats
  • Service Providers: With hosting providers (who are contractually obligated to protect your data)

Publicly Visible Information

Some data may be publicly visible by design:

  • Leaderboards: Your username and scores are visible to other users
  • Polls: Your vote may be visible depending on server settings
  • Game Results: Match results in public channels are visible to all users

🌍 International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country. By using Discorbo, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries that may have different data protection laws.

We ensure adequate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

📝 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting.

The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when the policy was last revised. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Material Changes: If we make significant changes that affect your rights, we will notify users via Discord announcements or in-app notifications.
